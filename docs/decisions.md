# Decisions and open questions

Questions raised while planning Phase 1 and the answers given (21 Oct 2026).

| #   | Question                                                                                    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Where in code                                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Gate in tap count: IC and document photos in one camera session so gate in stays at 3 taps? | **Yes.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Gate In screen (step 3)                                             |
| 2   | IC number: store the full IC or only the last 4 digits? No OCR in Phase 1.                  | **IC photo + last 4 digits only.** No full IC stored.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | `persons.ic_last4`                                                  |
| 3   | Which DO belongs to this truck when one SDO ships on several trucks?                        | **Yes** to the rule: a DO counts if it references a trip SDO, was created at or after gate in, and is not claimed by another trip; the clerk can link/unlink by hand. See also 3a. To be checked with logistics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `packages/domain/src/sdoDoLinking.ts`, `trip_dos_one_claim` index   |
| 3a  | How does a truck get its SDO?                                                               | **Two ways.** (1) **Pre-registered:** the clerk enters plate, SDO(s) and expected date before arrival; an exact plate match at gate in links the SDOs automatically. (2) **On the spot:** guard registers the truck at the gate; the clerk matches SDOs on the clerk screen. If the camera misreads a pre-registered plate, the guard picks the truck from the open pre-registration list, or registers it on the spot. Pre-registrations never disappear: after the expected date they show as **Late** until the truck arrives or the clerk clears them by hand. | `packages/domain/src/preRegistration.ts`, `pre_registrations` table |
| 4   | Outbound truck at exit with no SDO linked                                                   | **Red** + supervisor alert (option a). The system still does not block the truck (hard rule in CLAUDE.md); the guard holds it and calls the supervisor.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `EXIT_NO_SDO` in `packages/domain/src/alerts.ts`                    |
| 5   | IC photo retention until the PDPA period is set                                             | **90 days**, configurable (`IC_PHOTO_RETENTION_DAYS`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | `apps/server/src/config.ts`, retention job (step 9)                 |
| 6   | Watchlist (red at gate in)                                                                  | **In Phase 1.** Plates and transporters; supervisor adds and removes entries. Driver watchlist waits until drivers are identifiable (only last 4 IC digits are stored).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `WATCHLIST_HIT`, `watchlist` table                                  |
| 7   | Supervisor alerts in Phase 1                                                                | **On screen only.** WhatsApp later behind the `Notifier` adapter.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `packages/adapters/src/notify`                                      |
| 8   | How is a truck with 2 or more SDOs ticketed in CWIS?                                        | **One CWIS ticket can carry several SDOs.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | `CwisTicket.sdoNos`, `weigh_tickets.sdo_nos`                        |
| 9   | Checker: pieces that differ from the SDO                                                    | **Recorded, not refused** (provisional). On a split delivery a truck may carry only part of an SDO, so the checker can approve with different pieces; every difference is kept on the decision and in the audit log. Once DOs are captured (step 6) the expected pieces come from the DO where one exists. Confirm with logistics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `packages/domain/src/checker.ts`                                    |
| 10  | Checker rejects before counting every line                                                  | Unchecked lines are recorded as expected pieces (provisional); the reject reason is what matters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `apps/web/src/screens/Checker.tsx`                                  |

Open items from the spec (CWIS vendor, SAP partner, internal) stay in `docs/SPEC.md`.
