# Decisions and open questions

Questions raised while planning Phase 1 and the answers given (21 Oct 2026).

| #   | Question                                                                                    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Where in code                                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Gate in tap count: IC and document photos in one camera session so gate in stays at 3 taps? | **Yes.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Gate In screen (step 3)                                             |
| 2   | IC number: store the full IC or only the last 4 digits? No OCR in Phase 1.                  | **IC photo + last 4 digits only.** No full IC stored.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | `persons.ic_last4`                                                  |
| 3   | Which DO belongs to this truck when one SDO ships on several trucks?                        | **Yes** to the rule: a DO counts if it references a trip SDO, was created at or after gate in, and is not claimed by another trip; the clerk can link/unlink by hand. See also 3a. To be checked with logistics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `packages/domain/src/sdoDoLinking.ts`, `trip_dos_one_claim` index   |
| 3a  | How does a truck get its SDO?                                                               | **Two ways.** (1) **Pre-registered:** the clerk enters plate, SDO(s) and expected date before arrival; an exact plate match at gate in links the SDOs automatically. (2) **On the spot:** guard registers the truck at the gate; the clerk matches SDOs on the clerk screen. If the camera misreads a pre-registered plate, the guard picks the truck from the open pre-registration list, or registers it on the spot. Pre-registrations never disappear: after the expected date they show as **Late** until the truck arrives or the clerk clears them by hand. | `packages/domain/src/preRegistration.ts`, `pre_registrations` table |
| 4   | Outbound truck at exit with no SDO linked                                                   | **Red** + supervisor alert (option a). The system still does not block the truck (hard rule in CLAUDE.md); the guard holds it and calls the supervisor.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `EXIT_NO_SDO` in `packages/domain/src/alerts.ts`                    |
| 5   | IC photo retention until the PDPA period is set                                             | **90 days**, configurable (`IC_PHOTO_RETENTION_DAYS`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | `apps/server/src/config.ts`, retention job (step 9)                 |
| 6   | Watchlist (red at gate in)                                                                  | **In Phase 1.** Plates and transporters; supervisor adds and removes entries. Driver watchlist waits until drivers are identifiable (only last 4 IC digits are stored).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `WATCHLIST_HIT`, `watchlist` table                                  |
| 7   | Supervisor alerts in Phase 1                                                                | **On screen only.** WhatsApp later behind the `Notifier` adapter.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `packages/adapters/src/notify`                                      |
| 8   | How is a truck with 2 or more SDOs ticketed in CWIS?                                        | **One CWIS ticket can carry several SDOs.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | `CwisTicket.sdoNos`, `weigh_tickets.sdo_nos`                        |
| 9   | Checker: pieces that differ from the SDO                                                    | **Recorded, not refused** (provisional). On a split delivery a truck may carry only part of an SDO, so the checker can approve with different pieces; every difference is kept on the decision and in the audit log. Once DOs are captured (step 6) the expected pieces come from the DO where one exists. Confirm with logistics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `packages/domain/src/checker.ts`                                    |
| 10  | Checker rejects before counting every line                                                  | Unchecked lines are recorded as expected pieces (provisional); the reject reason is what matters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `apps/web/src/screens/Checker.tsx`                                  |
| 11  | A new DO fits two trucks on site (same SDO)                                                 | **Not guessed** (provisional): a DO links automatically only when exactly one truck fits (its SDO, created after that truck came in, not on another truck). Otherwise the clerk sees it on each truck and links it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | `assignDos` in `packages/domain/src/sdoDoLinking.ts`                |
| 12  | Clerk unlinks a DO already updated in SAP                                                   | UDFs already written **stay in SAP**; the VMS never clears SAP data. Updates not yet sent are dropped. The unlink is in the audit log. Confirm with the SAP partner whether they want the fields cleared.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `unlinkDo` in `apps/server/src/services/dos.ts`                     |
| 13  | Gate out without the card                                                                   | **Two taps** ("No card", then "Yes, no card") so a slip does not charge a fee. The card is marked lost, the trip's transporter gets +1 lost card (fee per spec), red alert. A lost card handed back later is returned to the pool with `POST /api/cards/:cardNo/recover`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `apps/server/src/services/gateOut.ts`                               |
| 14  | A DO made after the truck has left                                                          | Still linked automatically for **24 hours** after gate out (`LATE_DO_HOURS`), so SAP gets plate, ticket, checker and gate times. The exit alert stays as it was (history).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | `discoverDos` in `apps/server/src/services/dos.ts`                  |

## Visitors and contractors (29 Oct 2026)

Built after trucks at the owner's request. Staff are left for later except a plate log.

| #   | Question                                                    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Where in code                              |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| V1  | Who creates invites?                                        | **Office users** (clerk, supervisor, admin). Hosts inviting their own visitors comes later.                                                                                                                                                             | Office → Visitors tab                      |
| V2  | How does the visitor get the invite?                        | **"Share on WhatsApp"** opens the sender's own WhatsApp with the message (no WhatsApp Business API), and **"Copy QR"** copies the QR image to paste anywhere. The gate does not need the internet.                                                      | Office → Visitors tab                      |
| V3  | Walk-in host approval (hosts have no login)                 | The guard phones the host and taps **"Host OK"** (recorded with the guard's name). If the host cannot be reached the visitor still comes in, amber "No host OK" to the supervisor.                                                                      | `HOST_NOT_CONFIRMED`                       |
| V4  | Safety video and PPE agreement                              | **Not now** (no video yet).                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | —                                          |
| V5  | Contractor documents (induction, permit to work, insurance) | **No checks for now.** Contractors are handled like visitors and marked "Contractor".                                                                                                                                                                   | `visit_type`                               |
| V6  | Groups                                                      | **One person registers** and states how many people are with them. **One card** for the group; the headcount counts everyone.                                                                                                                           | `visits.group_size`                        |
| V7  | Kiosk                                                       | **Shares the entry guard tablet.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Gate In screen                             |
| V8  | Muster list                                                 | **Yes.** Live headcount of truck drivers, visitors and contractors; in an emergency the supervisor starts a roll call (fixed when it starts) and ticks people safe. Leaving more than 30 minutes after the invite's end time shows amber "Stayed late". | `packages/domain/src/visits.ts`, `musters` |
| V9  | Staff cars until staff is built                             | A **"Staff"** button at the gate logs the plate and time. No card, no photos.                                                                                                                                                                           | `staff_entries`                            |
| V10 | Visitor IC photo retention                                  | **90 days**, same as drivers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | `IC_PHOTO_RETENTION_DAYS`                  |

Visits have their own table rather than sharing `trips`: a truck trip needs a vehicle and feeds SDO, CWIS and SAP logic that does not apply to people.

Open items from the spec (CWIS vendor, SAP partner, internal) stay in `docs/SPEC.md`.
